# Fetal DS directional specificity analysis

This plan was recorded before computing new donor-level estimates. Archived group summaries for DNMT3L were available when it was written.

- Primary cohort: GSE74486 fetal cerebrum, normalized beta values before ComBat, independent donor per GEO annotation. Fit the DS minus control beta coefficient across CpGs, adjusting for sex and any verifiable, estimable prenatal age and batch information. Use GSE73747 fetal cortex for a separate assessment with gestational age, sex and region. Resolve repeated GSE73747 arrays with documented identifiers or array SNP-probe concordance, then collapse within donor and region and keep regions from the same donor together when resampling.
- Lock the GSE157078 DNMT3L OE signature to the archived `|Δβ| ≥ 0.05` calls. Select autosomal CpGs covered and callable in both assays, with manifest mapping/SNP QC and detection P ≤ 0.01 in the sample matrices. Use the same eligibility for all analyses within a cohort. Conventional bisulfite arrays measure combined modified cytosine signal.
- Use the Mantel–Haenszel OR of DS hypermethylation for DNMT3L hyper versus hypo probes. Strata are chromosome, island category, probe design, and 0.2-wide bins of label-blind mean fetal beta. Compute 95% percentile intervals by resampling independent donors within diagnosis and region pattern, refitting the per-probe regression each time. Inspect leave-one-donor-out results and thresholds of 0.01 and 0.02 on the absolute fitted DS beta change. Exact zero changes are excluded from the 2×2 tables.
- For the matched-CpG null, sample other eligible probes without replacement within each stratum, preserving DNMT3L hyper/hypo counts. Contrast the observed OR with 2,000 balanced random panels and obtain a paired donor-bootstrap interval for the ratio to the OR computed from expected matched-panel counts. The empirical upper-tail probability conditions on the cohort and matched-locus design.
- For the cross-tissue comparator, derive a human DNMT3A W330R gain-of-function signature from the GSE120558 fibroblast EPIC matrix. Average technical replicates before comparing the two variant donors with the healthy fibroblast controls. Require each variant donor to agree in direction and a mean absolute effect of at least 0.05. Restrict to probes callable in all three assays. Balance direction, the four OR strata and 0.05-wide absolute perturbation-effect bins (top bin ≥0.15) between signatures. Use all common support, with equal expected counts via within-cell sampling weights. Recompute both ORs on the same resampled DS donors. A cross-tissue contrast measures relative transferability in this design.
- Anchor inference to effect sizes and uncertainty conditional on the external signature estimates. Treat cohort heterogeneity, limited overlap after balancing, and comparator tissue differences as constraints on interpretation. Donor label permutations require defensible exchangeability and are secondary to the bootstrap intervals.
